# ADR 0005 Decision 2 names two decimation filters; the publisher needs a third, and SLC-28 may reorder them

- **Reported by**: slicer (`ws/slicer`, SLC-14, on top of `ca7ff1d`)
- **Affects**: release — `docs/adr/0005-slicer-glb-export-coordinates-and-node-ids.md` (Decision 2: "`vtkQuadricDecimation`,
  fallback `vtkDecimatePro(PreserveTopologyOn)`")
- **Severity**: low
- **Milestone**: M2
- **Protocol**: none (§7.5 budgets unchanged)
- **Status**: open — the rule is in (SLC-52, below); the ADR erratum is release's

## Steps to reproduce

1. `remoteview_core.decimate.FILTERS` (SLC-14): `vtkQuadricDecimation`, then `vtkDecimatePro(PreserveTopologyOn)`, then
   `vtkDecimatePro` with its defaults (topology changes, splitting and boundary deletion allowed).
2. Decimate a dense, exactly flat 0.1 m plane of 180k triangles, or an unsmoothed segmentation surface with flat caps
   (427k triangles), to 100k with the ADR's first filter.

## Expected

ADR 0005 Decision 2: quadric decimation, falling back to `vtkDecimatePro(PreserveTopologyOn)`; every node within the
§7.5 per-node cap ("decimated further, never dropped").

## Actual

- `vtkDecimatePro(PreserveTopologyOn)` may stop short of the target (VTK documents it, `vtkDecimatePro.h` L96–L106), so a
  node could stay above the cap; SLC-14 adds `vtkDecimatePro`'s defaults as a third filter, for which VTK documents a
  guaranteed reduction (not reached for duplicated or non-manifold triangles — then the node is sent whole and
  reported). `docs/research/slicer-decimate.md`.
- The quadric is super-linear on dense, exactly flat regions: 57 s (flat plane → 100k), 437 s and 3 GB (flat-capped
  segmentation surface → 100k), where `vtkDecimatePro(PreserveTopologyOn)` needs ≈ 3 s — Slicer's main thread is frozen
  meanwhile. SLC-28 must bound one node's time (plan criterion 2), which may need `vtkDecimatePro` first for such
  input — a change of the ADR's order.

## Notes / suspected cause

Please add an erratum to ADR 0005 Decision 2: "quadric, then `vtkDecimatePro(PreserveTopologyOn)`, then
`vtkDecimatePro` with topology changes allowed; the order may change where the quadric's time is unbounded (flat or
non-manifold input), see SLC-28". SLC-28 will add its rule to this report or a new one.

## The rule (SLC-52)

`remoteview_core.decimate` now decimates by this rule (`docs/research/slicer-decimate.md`, "One node in bounded time").
Proposed erratum to ADR 0005 Decision 2:

> Decimation input: coincident points merged (`vtkCleanPolyData`, tolerance 0) — a triangle soup always, other input
> unless the merged mesh has more edges of more than two triangles than the input (sheets lying on each other) —,
> centred, and jittered by 1e-7 of the bounding-box diagonal (seeded from a digest of the mesh). Manifold input:
> `vtkQuadricDecimation`, then `vtkDecimatePro(PreserveTopologyOn)`, then `vtkDecimatePro` with its defaults. Input with
> an edge shared by more than two triangles: `vtkDecimatePro(PreserveTopologyOn)` first, then the manifold chain on the
> mesh as it is while the quadric's set-up fits a budget, then on a copy whose shared edges' ends are cut. A node no
> filter reduces is sent whole and reported (§7.5).

Measured: the flat 180k plane 0.85–1.0 s (was > 90 s), the flat-capped 435k labelmap surface 3.6 s (was > 90 s), the
3,000-page book 0.08 s (four rows 1.1 s, were 18 s and > 90 s), a smooth 400k sphere ≈ 1.06× its time before, a triangle
soup without holes, a two-sided sphere as before (one side's area). Not bounded yet: a node of ≈ 1M triangles or more
(> 10 s whichever filter; 0.8M: 6.6–8.8 s; SLC-56), a node whose bounding box one far-off triangle stretches (the jitter
grows with the box: up to ≈ 30 s with a triangle 10 km away; SLC-56) and a point used by thousands of triangles — a fan,
a bow-tie, a book of more than ≈ 20,000 pages on one edge (SLC-55).
