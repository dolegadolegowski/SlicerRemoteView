## What changes for the relay

1. **`begin.byteLength` has no schema maximum (PRO-21, `todo`).** After PRO-21 lands, `parseEnvelope` no longer
   answers `INVALID_MESSAGE` for `byteLength > 64 MiB`; the clamp-before-validation in `snapshot.ts` (report
   2026-10-20-server-2) can be dropped and `PAYLOAD_TOO_LARGE{details.maxSnapshotBytes}` answered straight from the
   rule. `sceneVersion`/`snapshotVersion`/`updateVersion` get `maximum: 4294967295` in the schema: the relay's own
   uint32 guard for `/sceneVersion` becomes redundant (keep it as defence in depth if you like).
2. **Version constant (PRO-22, `todo`).** `PROTOCOL_VERSION` becomes `"1.1"`. `/api/version` `protocolVersions` and the
   `UNSUPPORTED_VERSION` details come from the constant; please decide whether to list `["1.1"]` (recommended: the
   relay accepts every `1.x`, §2) or `["1.0","1.1"]`, and update the tests that pin `"1.0"`. Peers announcing `1.0` or `1.1`
   are both accepted.
3. **TRUST_PROXY (D6, waiting for DEP-06).** §12 now says "client address as determined by the relay's trusted-proxy
   configuration". If the first deploy shows more than one hop, replace the boolean by a hop-count/trusted-list
   function (plan note SRV-02). Nothing to do before the measurement.

4. **Not yet in the text (PRO-25, `todo`).** Your reports 2026-10-27-server-3/4/5 (per-socket budget, resume
   details, draining `503`) are triaged but not written into `docs/protocol.md` yet. Review follow-ups for you to
   decide with us: restart the `4012` timer at most once per socket (an unauthenticated socket that sends
   `session.resume` every 9 s currently stays open), a minimum drain rate for "send buffer shrank" counting as
   life, a coarse limiter for refused upgrades, a bound on the ended viewer-token hashes. Log the request path
   only, never the query string (`/j?code=` answers 200, §10).
