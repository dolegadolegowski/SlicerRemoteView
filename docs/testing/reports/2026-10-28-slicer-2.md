- **Reported by**: slicer (`ws/slicer-perf`, SLC-41, on top of `86f85bc`)
- **Affects**: release — `docs/architecture.md` §6 step 4 ("fallback `vtkDecimatePro(PreserveTopologyOn)` if
  degenerate"), `docs/adr/0005-slicer-glb-export-coordinates-and-node-ids.md` Decision 2 ("fallback
  `vtkDecimatePro(PreserveTopologyOn)`"); supersedes the erratum text requested in `2026-10-21-slicer-4`
- **Severity**: medium (the documents describe a pipeline that is no longer used, and the requested erratum would be
  wrong)
- **Milestone**: M2
- **Protocol**: none (§7.4 normals with `SplittingOff` and §7.5 budgets, "never dropped", are unchanged)
- **Status**: open

## Steps to reproduce

1. Read `docs/architecture.md` §6 step 4 and ADR 0005 Decision 2.
2. Read `slicer-plugin/RemoteView/remoteview_core/decimate.py` (SLC-41) and `docs/research/slicer-decimate.md`
   § "Very large meshes and the stall (SLC-41)".

## Expected

The architecture and the ADR describe the decimation the publisher runs.

## Actual

A user's 16.2M-triangle micro-CT segmentation froze Slicer for about 3 minutes, and the phone showed cracks, shards
and large flat facets: `vtkQuadricDecimation` stalled (a NaN cost from boundary edges that are zero-length in its
float32 copy of the points), `vtkDecimatePro(PreserveTopologyOn)` missed the share, and `vtkDecimatePro` with its
defaults made the result — its splitting cracked the surface. Since SLC-41 the pipeline is:

- a node above `2 · max(400k, 3 · share)` triangles is first reduced by connected vertex clustering (numpy; a cluster
  is a connected piece of the mesh in one grid cell, so both sides of a thin wall stay apart; seams stay closed) to
  at most twice that goal;
- a mesh with non-manifold fans (`fan_weight` > 50, or a quadric set-up `edge_work` > 2e9) is clustered by grid cell
  and normal class;
- parts below 2,000 triangles get their own quadric run;
- `vtkQuadricDecimation` runs on centred coordinates scaled to a median edge of 100, after welding edge ends that
  coincide in float32, with `RegularizeOn` (0.01) and one 8 s deadline per node for its quadric runs;
- if any part fails: clustering of the whole node with a growing cell (connected clusters, then normal classes);
- folds are turned over, and normals come from `vtkPolyDataNormals` with `SplittingOff` and `ConsistencyOff`;
- `vtkDecimatePro` is not used; a node nothing can reduce is still kept whole and reported (never dropped).
